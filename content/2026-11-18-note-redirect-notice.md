title: Why Gmail stopped my download button
date: 2026-11-18
type: note
tags: email, lessons
---
I sent friends a launch email from my personal Gmail, and the download button hit a Google "Redirect Notice" first. Gmail doesn't fully trust a link to a week-old domain in mail from a personal address. Two fixes: the button now opens a real welcome page that starts the download itself, and launch emails will come from hello@freshflo.online with the domain verified.
