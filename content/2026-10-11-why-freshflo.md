Freshflo began as a side project called **ExcuseMe**: a Mac app that let me talk instead of type. On 11 October 2026 it got a new name, a new icon and a public beta.

The icon tells the whole idea: a messy voice line that settles into a clean stroke and a full stop. You say it messy; Freshflo hands back the version you meant.

## Why a blog

Most product pages show the finished thing. This blog is the other side: the bugs, the measurements, the decisions and the dead ends. If you use Freshflo, this is where you'll see why something changed.

## What to expect

- Notes on each release, with the numbers behind them
- Bugs that took far too long to find
- Decisions still open, and what I'm weighing

If something here is wrong or you want a deeper dive, [write to me](https://freshflo.vercel.app/help#ask). A person reads every message.
